# Bio Page Plan - Standalone Offering at $3/Month

## Overview

Added a new standalone **"Bio Page"** plan at **$3/month** targeting users who only need the digital identity/link-in-bio feature without requiring the full URL shortening capabilities.

## Strategic Rationale

### Market Opportunity
- **Linktree** charges $5-$9/month for similar bio page features
- **Beacons** charges $10/month for their link-in-bio service
- **Stan Store** charges $29/month for creator pages
- **Your Bio Page**: $3/month = **40-90% cheaper** than competitors

### Target Audience
1. **Content Creators** (YouTubers, TikTokers, Podcasters)
2. **Influencers** (Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn)
3. **Musicians & Artists**
4. **Freelancers & Consultants**
5. **Small Business Owners**
6. **Personal Branding Enthusiasts**

### Why This Works

**Unbundling Strategy**:
- Some users only want a bio page, not URL shortening
- $3/month is an impulse buy price point
- Lower barrier to entry than $19/month Professional plan
- Can upsell to Professional later if they need link management

**Competitive Positioning**:
- Linktree Free: Very limited (3 links, basic features)
- Linktree Starter: $5/month
- **Your Bio Page**: $3/month with MORE features

## Pricing Structure

### Monthly
- **$3/month** - Simple, affordable, impulse buy

- **$28.80/year** ($2.40/month)
